FAQs

How to book? Cancel? Refund?

How to book?
Go to Book page → select check-in / check-out → choose your pet → Confirm Booking. You'll get a confirmation email instantly with your host details and stay summary.
How to cancel?
You can cancel free within 24h of booking. Go to My Bookings → Cancel. After 24h, a 50% fee applies to support our hosts. Emergencies are always reviewed case-by-case.
Refund policy?
Full refund if cancelled 48h before check-in. 50% refund within 24-48h. No refund for no-show. For disrupted stays, contact us within 24h and we'll make it right.
